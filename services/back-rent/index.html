The most misunderstood number

The $3,500 is a cap on recovery, not a limit on who qualifies.

This trips up almost every landlord, so let's be exact about it.

What $3,500 is not

It is not an eligibility ceiling. A tenant who owes more than $3,500 is not disqualified. You don't lose access because the balance is large.

What $3,500 is

It's the maximum back rent the program will recover. If the balance is higher, you settle the back-due portion at the $3,500 cap, and you still receive the two future months on top.

Worked example. A tenant owes you $5,800. You're not shut out. The program settles the back rent at the $3,500 cap and still pays two months of future rent by direct deposit. Getting most of it, plus a runway of future rent, beats carrying the full loss and paying to evict.
