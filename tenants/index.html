Information for tenants

We work for landlords — but you're not out of options.

Rental Assistance Philadelphia is a private filing service hired by property owners. We can't file for you, and we don't want you to spend time on a form that won't help. Below are the free Philadelphia services that do help renters directly. Please use them — they cost nothing, and they are the right people.

The one thing worth knowing first

Philadelphia's Eviction Diversion Program can pay past-due rent directly to your landlord — up to $3,500, plus two months of future rent. It is real money and it is free to apply for.

But the landlord files it, not you. The City's Eviction Diversion Program application is submitted by the property owner. That is why we can't do it for you — and it is why the most useful thing you can do is get your landlord to apply. The tenant hotline below can explain the program to you and help you raise it with them.

Questions we get from tenants

Can you apply for rental assistance for me?

No. We are hired by property owners to prepare and manage their filings. The Eviction Diversion Program application is filed by the landlord, so there is nothing we can file on your behalf.

Can you talk to my landlord for me?

No — we only act for owners who have engaged us. The Eviction Diversion Program tenant hotline (215-523-9501) can explain the program to you so you can raise it with your landlord yourself.

Does it cost anything to apply to the City's program?

No. The City's Eviction Diversion Program and Targeted Financial Assistance are free to apply for. Our fee is charged to owners who hire us to handle their paperwork — never to tenants.

Is this website the City?

No. Rental Assistance Philadelphia is a brand of Rental Assistance Services LLC, a private company. We are not a government agency, not affiliated with the City of Philadelphia, and not a law firm.
