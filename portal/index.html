Rental Assistance Philadelphia is a brand of Rental Assistance Services LLC, a private, independent filing service hired by property owners. We do not represent tenants and cannot file on a tenant's behalf — tenants, please see where to get help. We are not a government agency and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the City of Philadelphia, the Philadelphia Housing Development Corporation, or any government entity, and we are not a law firm. You can apply for the City's Eviction Diversion Program / Targeted Financial Assistance directly with the City yourself at no cost — our success-based fee (33% of the funds the City pays) is for preparing and managing the filing on your behalf. We do not provide legal advice. Program details (Ordinance #220655 / Phila. Code § 9-811) reflect the City's Eviction Diversion Program; verify specifics at the City's official portal. The $3,500 figure is the cap on recoverable back rent, not an eligibility ceiling. Outcome figures reflect real diversion filings managed to date; results vary by case.
